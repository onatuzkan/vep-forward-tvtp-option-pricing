## Commentary and corrections

**F14 is a single-regime reduction of a two-regime model.**  The iso-variance curves collapse the regime-mixture stationary variance onto one shared Gaussian sigma per kappa, and price a single-regime OU option under that Gaussian.  The two-regime production process at the same total variance has heavier tails than a Gaussian (kurtosis roughly 5-7 versus 3), and a fatter-tailed centered distribution reduces the ATM call value at a fixed sd (the ATM value is proportional to the interior mass, not the tails).  This is why the 72 h ATM call at production is 166.75 while the production-targeted iso-variance curve at kappa 0.078 gives 183.4 -- same variance, thinner tails, higher ATM.  The earlier "reproduce within a few percent" claim was wrong and has been corrected in the paragraph above.

**Read F14 as a shape statement, not a level statement.**  The production-targeted iso-variance curve moves by 8 pct across the model-faithful kappa family kappa in [0.15, 0.22] (call_T72 in the 169-176 band) and by roughly 15 pct across the full [0.01, 0.30] range (162-187).  Over the same kappa range the T = 1 h ATM call moves from 7 to 98 TRY -- shape sensitivity to kappa dwarfs the sensitivity of long-horizon prices.  The single-regime iso-variance abstraction is therefore useful for reading the kappa-versus-tenor shape trade-off, not for endorsing or rejecting a specific level.

**ATM prices scale approximately linearly, not quadratically, with sd.**  Under Bachelier at ATM the call value is (sd) * sqrt(2 * tau / pi).  Comparing the three iso-variance targets at kappa 0.078: production (sd 582.48) -> 167.09, observed_2025_TRY (sd 624.0) -> 181.58, observed_2025H2_TRY (sd 556.0) -> 157.87.  The ratios 181.58 / 167.09 = 1.087 and 157.87 / 167.09 = 0.945 track the sd ratios 624 / 582.48 = 1.071 and 556 / 582.48 = 0.955 (deviations at the second decimal come from the finite horizon and non-Gaussian tails, not from a quadratic relationship).

**Full-window consistent fit's TRY sd of 927 is a regime-pooling artefact.**  The FW9e A3 fit stationary sd on the delta-mapped TRY scale is roughly 984 TRY (see section 4 tail_validation), well above the observed 2025 sd 531 or 2025-H2 sd 556.  Pooling 2019-2021 (low-price regime, average PTF around 400-500 TRY/MWh with low absolute variance) with 2022-2025 (average around 2000-3000 TRY/MWh with much higher absolute variance) on the asinh scale forces the fit to inflate sigma_stress to fit both eras; the 2022-2025-only regime-matched fit reduces this only partially (stationary sd 1176 -- see section 4).  The delta mapping at a single spot F does not undo this inflation.

**+25 pct ATM vs +312 pct at K = 4000 comes from the mixture kurtosis.**  Under the production two-regime process, a 25 pct sigma_stress uplift raises the 72 h ATM call by roughly 25 pct (linear in sd, above), but shifts a K = 4000 tail call by more than 300 pct because that strike lives in the mixture's high-kurtosis tail; the same uplift moves the tail probability mass much more than the interior.  The excess kurtosis of the production mixture at the 72 h horizon is roughly 3-5 above Gaussian; that number scales the tail-versus-centre asymmetry directly.

**The asinh plus delta mapping has a structural upper bound.**  With scale_P frozen at 282.48 TRY/MWh (documented ASSUMED provenance) the delta at F = 2917.78 is fixed at 2931.42 TRY per unit asinh residual.  Any two-regime asinh residual with stationary sd 0.20 maps to a TRY sd of about 583, regardless of kappa or transition matrix; only the mixture shape (kurtosis, skew) can move at fixed sd, and F14's iso-variance reduction discards exactly that.  Reading levels off F14 therefore over-attributes the ATM price to sd and hides the mixture-shape channel.
