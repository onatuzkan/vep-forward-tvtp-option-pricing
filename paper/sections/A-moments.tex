\section{Derivation of the moment system}
\label{app:moments}

Let $f_i(x,t)$ be the joint density of $(X_t,S_t=i)$ under $\Q$, with $X$
following \eqref{eq:ou} and the chain generated by $Q(t)$, possibly
transformed as in \eqref{eq:q2}. The forward equation for the pair is
\begin{equation}
  \partial_t f_i
  = -\partial_x\!\left[\left(\kappa(m_i-x)+a_i\right)f_i\right]
    + \tfrac12\left(\sigma^X_i\right)^2\partial_{xx}f_i
    + q_{ji}f_j - q_{ij}f_i,\qquad j\neq i.
\end{equation}
Multiplying by $1$, $x$ and $x^2$ and integrating over $\mathbb{R}$, with
boundary terms vanishing because $f_i$ and $xf_i$ decay at infinity, gives
\eqref{eq:momentsys} for $p_i=\int f_i\,\dif x$, $u_i=\int xf_i\,\dif x$ and
$w_i=\int x^2f_i\,\dif x$. Time dependence enters through $q_{ij}(t)$ along
the covariate path and through $\sigma^X_i(t)$ via \eqref{eq:sigmamap}.

For a drift shift of size $a$, write $u_i=u_i^{(0)}+a\,u_i^{(1)}+O(a^2)$. The
$u$ block gains the forcing $a_ip_i$ and is first order in $a$; the $w$ block
gains $2a_iu_i$, so the change in variance, $w_0+w_1-\mu_X^2$, is of order
$a^2$. This concerns the variance channel. For a contract away from the money
the regime reweighting induced by $a_i$ can contribute a first-order term to
the option value, and the numerical results of Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}
bound the total effect rather than establishing a general second-order result.
A change of intensities, by contrast, enters the $p$ block directly and moves
the stationary occupancy, and hence the variance, at first order in $\eta$.
