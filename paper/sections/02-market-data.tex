\section{Market, contract and data}
\label{sec:market}

Wholesale electricity in Türkiye is cleared by EPİAŞ. The day-ahead market
sets an hourly price, the \emph{Piyasa Takas Fiyatı} (PTF), for each hour of
the following day, and the auction results for day $D+1$ are published at
about 14:00 local time (TRT, UTC+3) on day $D$ \citep{EPIAS2025}. Prices are
quoted in Turkish lira per megawatt-hour and are bounded by administratively
set floor and ceiling levels \citep{EPDK2024}. The forward segment, VEP, lists
monthly baseload futures that settle against the arithmetic mean of PTF over
all hours of the delivery month. A quote therefore restricts the average of
672 to 744 hourly forward prices and is silent on how that average is
distributed across hours, which is the first source of the identification
problem studied here.

\subsection{The contract}

The instrument valued is a European call on the PTF of a single expiry hour
$T$,
\begin{equation}
  V_0 = e^{-rT}\,\E^{\Q}\!\left[(P_T-K)^{+}\right],
  \label{eq:payoff}
\end{equation}
with strike $K$ and a flat continuously compounded rate $r$. The payoff is
written on one hourly price and not on the monthly average that VEP settles,
and the two are kept apart in the implementation: a monthly quote cannot be
read as a point forward.

\subsection{Valuation instant and samples}
\label{sec:timing}

Because day-ahead prices are published a day in advance, the valuation instant
has to be placed so that no payoff is already known. Valuation is set at 11:00
TRT on 31 December 2025. At that time the last published price is that of
31 December, 23:00 TRT, which equals $2{,}917.78$\,\TRY{} and serves as the
observed spot, and all maturities are counted from that hour. The forward
curve is built from the last VEP reference prices published before 11:00, those
of 30 December. They are identical to the quotes of 31 December for every
contract, so the curve, and every option value reported below, coincides with
a construction that uses the year-end quotes.

Six monthly contracts were quoted, for delivery from February to July 2026
(Table~\ref{tab:quotes}); January carried no quote. The strip falls from
February to June and then rises by 58\% into July, a seasonal step that the
hourly curve has to accommodate.

Three further samples are used. Hourly PTF from January 2019 to the valuation
hour (61{,}368 observations) is used to re-estimate the residual process in
Section~\ref{sec:estimation}. The transition covariate is hourly residual
demand, load net of wind and solar output, standardised with the scaler of the
original estimation sample. Realised PTF from 1 January to 27 September 2026
serves only for evaluation. No observation after the valuation cutoff enters
any calibration, estimation or model choice, and this is enforced by tests
that fail if a fitting routine is given a later timestamp.
